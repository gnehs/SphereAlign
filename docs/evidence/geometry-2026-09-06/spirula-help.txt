Spirula Studio 2026.9.2 (4361a41)

  spirula                        open the application
  spirula <file-or-folder>       open it in the application
  spirula <command> [options]

Commands:
  gui      the graphical application (the default)
  sfm      structure from motion: photos or frames -> cameras
  train    train a splat model on a dataset
  sam      segmentation, tracking and frame extraction
  geometry estimate depth and surface normals for a dataset
  mesh     extract a mesh from a trained model

`spirula <command> --help` describes one of them.

Every command also takes --lang <code>, which sets the interface language (or SS_LANG in the environment):

    en        English
    ja        Japanese
    zh-Hans   Chinese (Simplified)
    zh-Hant   Chinese (Traditional)
    ko        Korean
    de        German
    fr        French
    es        Spanish
    pt        Portuguese
    it        Italian
    nl        Dutch
    ru        Russian
    tr        Turkish
